Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Lord Krishna Public School, Meerut may collect, use, store, and protect information shared through this website or during an admission enquiry.

  • Effective date: 18 September 2026
  • Applies to: Website visitors and admission enquiries

1. Information we may collect

We may collect information that a parent or guardian chooses to share with the school, including:

  • name of the parent, guardian, or student;
  • phone number and other contact details;
  • class interest or admission-related details; and
  • other information shared during a phone call, visit, or enquiry.

2. How we use information

Information may be used to:

  • respond to admission or general school enquiries;
  • share basic information about classes, eligibility, or the admission process;
  • maintain school records where needed for legitimate school administration; and
  • improve the accuracy and usefulness of information published on the website.

3. Sharing of information

The school does not sell personal information. Information may be shared only where reasonably necessary for school administration, website operation, legal compliance, or to protect the rights and safety of the school, students, and families.

4. Cookies and basic website data

This website may use standard server logs, browser data, or analytics-style information such as device type, browser type, pages visited, and approximate usage patterns. This helps maintain the website and understand how visitors use it.

5. Data security

The school takes reasonable steps to protect information from unauthorised access, misuse, or disclosure. No website or digital system can guarantee absolute security, so visitors should avoid sending highly sensitive information through unsecured channels.

6. Retention of information

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for enquiries, school administration, record keeping, legal obligations, or related legitimate purposes.

7. Children's information

Any information relating to a student should be shared by a parent, guardian, or authorised family member. The website is intended for parents and guardians seeking information about the school.

8. Your choices

To update, correct, or request removal of information that you have shared with the school, please contact the school directly using the details on the Contact Us page. The school may keep limited records where required for lawful or administrative reasons.

9. Updates to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in school operations, website features, or legal requirements. The latest version published on this page will apply.
