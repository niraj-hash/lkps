Refund Policy

This Refund Policy outlines the general position of Lord Krishna Public School, Meerut, in relation to admission-related and fee-related payments.

  • Effective date: 18 September 2026
  • Applies to: School payments and admission enquiries

1. General policy

Payments made to the school are generally treated as final once they have been accepted and recorded. This includes amounts paid towards admission processing, registration, and school fees, unless the school explicitly confirms otherwise in writing.

2. Admission and registration amounts

Admission-related amounts, including enquiry processing, registration, or seat-booking amounts, are normally non-refundable because they are linked to administrative work and seat planning.

3. Tuition fees and other school charges

Tuition fees and other school charges already paid for an academic period are generally non-refundable. Any exceptional consideration remains at the sole discretion of the school management and will depend on the circumstances of the case.

4. Duplicate or incorrect payments

If a parent or guardian believes a duplicate payment or an incorrect amount has been paid, the matter should be reported to the school promptly with payment details. After verification, the school may decide on an appropriate correction or refund where justified.

5. Approved refunds

Where the school approves a refund, the amount may be processed only after internal verification of the payment record and the claimant's identity. The school may request reasonable supporting information before completing the refund.

6. Processing time

Approved refunds, if any, are typically processed within a reasonable administrative period. Timelines may vary depending on the mode of payment, bank processing time, and the availability of supporting documents.

7. How to request a review

For refund-related questions, parents or guardians should contact the school directly by phone or visit the school office with payment details and supporting records.

Important: This page provides the school's general refund position. Any exception, adjustment, or goodwill refund remains subject to verification and the decision of the school management.
