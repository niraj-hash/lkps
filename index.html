About us

Welcome to Lord Krishna Public School

Lord Krishna Public School has been part of Shiv Sarover, on Garh Road in Meerut, since 2000. We teach children from Nursery through to Class 8, following the UP Board (UPMSP), affiliation number 1173.

A child can start with us in pre-primary and stay until middle school. Those years matter to us: we come to know each child and each family well, and we watch a student grow from a first day in Nursery to the year they leave us in Class 8.

26 years of teaching in Meerut, since 2000.

We want every child to feel safe, known and encouraged at school. Alongside a steady foundation in reading, writing and numbers, we make room for sport, art, music and celebration, because children learn from these too. That is the promise in our motto: learning that stays with a child for life.
